## The idea

A great sales conversation is only as good as what happens next: accurate notes, an updated CRM, and a follow-up that confirms what was agreed. These are exactly the tasks that slip when sellers are busy.

AI meeting tools and general assistants can now turn a **transcript or your rough notes** into a **structured summary**, **CRM-ready fields** and a **follow-up email** in minutes.

## Why it matters

- **Nothing falls through the cracks.** Next steps with owners and dates get captured every time.
- **Better data, better decisions.** Pipeline reviews and forecasts (Day 15) are only as good as the CRM.
- **Faster follow-up.** A same-day email confirming next steps signals professionalism and keeps momentum.

## How it works

1. **Get consent first.** Tell participants the call is recorded or transcribed. Recording laws vary: some places require everyone's consent. Follow local law and your company's policy, and use approved tools.
2. **Capture the call.** Use your meeting tool's transcript (Teams, Zoom and Google Meet all offer AI features), or paste your own rough notes.
3. **Summarise in your format.** "Summarise in this format: needs, decision makers, budget and timing, objections, next steps (owner and date). Flag anything unclear."
4. **Check, log, follow up.** Compare it with your memory, fix anything wrong, update the CRM and send the follow-up the same day.

## Real-world example

At **Lumen Technologies**, Microsoft's customer story describes staff using Copilot before meetings, to summarise what they need to know, and after meetings they couldn't attend, asking it what they missed and who they need to follow up with. Lumen's revenue chief values the time AI gives back to sellers at four hours per week each.

*An illustrative scenario* shows the habit in practice: a sales manager asks her team to use one five-field summary format for every customer call. Within a month, pipeline reviews take half the time, because every opportunity has the same clear facts: what the customer needs, who decides, and what happens next.

**The lesson for leaders:** set the consent rule and the summary format once, and let AI do the typing, with a human check before anything is saved or sent.
