## The idea

For many leaders, email is a second job. AI can now **summarise long threads**, help you **triage** what needs you, and **draft replies** in your voice. These features are built into **Copilot in Outlook** and **Gemini in Gmail**, and any general assistant can do the same with pasted text.

## Why it matters

- **Email is a big share of the week.** Microsoft's 2023 Work Trend Index found the heaviest email users (the top 25%) spend **8.8 hours a week** on email.
- **Focus time is scarce.** In the same study, 68% of people said they don't have enough uninterrupted focus time.
- **Long threads hide decisions.** A good summary surfaces what was agreed and what's still open.

## How it works

1. **Summarise long threads.** In Outlook, use Copilot's summary at the top of a thread. In Gmail, ask Gemini to summarise. Or paste into an approved assistant. Ask for "decisions, open questions, what I need to do by when."
2. **Triage each morning.** Ask: "Group my unread emails into: needs my decision, needs my reply, FYI only." Where your tools support it, this can cover the whole inbox.
3. **Draft replies in your voice.** Give the gist ("say yes to Tuesday, ask for the budget first") and your voice card (Day 7). Edit before sending.
4. **Know what to write yourself.** Bad news, feedback on performance, condolences: where the care is the point, use your own words.

**Which tool?** Microsoft 365 users: Copilot in Outlook. Google Workspace users: Gemini in Gmail. Neither? Paste into ChatGPT or Claude, removing names and confidential details first (Day 5).

## Real-world example

Microsoft's **2023 Work Trend Index** surveyed 31,000 people in 31 countries and analysed Microsoft 365 usage data. It found the average employee spends **57% of their time communicating** (in meetings, email and chat) and only 43% creating. The researchers call the overload "digital debt": more email, meetings and notifications than people can process.

*An illustrative scenario* shows the fix in practice: a regional director starts each day by asking Copilot what needs her decision or reply. She drafts most routine answers with AI and writes the sensitive ones herself. Her email time drops from about two hours a day to under one.

**The lesson for leaders:** treat email as a task for AI-assisted triage and drafting, and keep your own voice for what matters most.
