## The idea

Understanding competitors and markets used to mean days of searching, reading and summarising. AI **research features**, often called "deep research", can now search many sources, read them and produce a **documented report** in minutes.

That's a genuine leap. But a polished report with citations can still contain misread numbers, outdated facts or weak sources. The skill is **using AI to gather, and humans to verify**.

## Why it matters

- **Faster, broader research.** A first view of a market or competitor in minutes, not days.
- **Better-prepared teams.** Sales, marketing and strategy can all work from the same sourced brief.
- **Decisions rest on facts.** If a wrong figure drives a pricing or investment decision, the cost is real.

## How it works

1. **Ask a focused research question.** "Research [competitor]: offer, target customers, pricing if public, recent news, strengths and weaknesses."
2. **Require sources.** "Cite a source for every claim. Separate facts from opinions. Flag anything uncertain." In ChatGPT and Gemini you can review the research plan before it starts.
3. **Read the report critically.** Which claims would change a decision? Those are the ones to check.
4. **Check the sources.** Open at least three. Confirm the number, the date and what it actually measures.

**Remember Day 5:** in Mata v. Avianca, lawyers were sanctioned for citing court cases ChatGPT had invented. Today's research tools link to real pages, but the principle stands: **check before you rely on it**.

## Real-world example

*An illustrative scenario.* A consumer goods company is considering entering the premium pet-food market. The strategy head runs a deep research task and gets a 12-page report in under 15 minutes: market size estimates, main brands, price ranges, regulation and trends.

She checks the five claims that matter most. Four hold up. One market-size figure turns out to come from a vendor's press release, not an independent study, and covers a different region. She replaces it with a better source before the board paper. The research took an afternoon instead of two weeks, and it's still trustworthy.

**The lesson for leaders:** let AI do the gathering. Make "we checked the sources that matter" a standard line in every research summary.
