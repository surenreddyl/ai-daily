## The idea

Buyers have little patience for sellers who haven't done their homework. But proper account research (reading the website, news, reports and LinkedIn) takes hours most sellers don't have.

AI can compress that work into minutes: you gather the sources, and it turns them into a **one-page brief** and a few **smart opening questions**. You stay responsible for checking what it says.

## Why it matters

- **First impressions are made in minutes.** A seller who knows the buyer's priorities earns the right to a second meeting.
- **Time goes back to selling.** Research is necessary but isn't selling. AI shrinks it.
- **Consistency.** Every rep can walk in equally prepared, not just the best researchers.

## How it works

1. **Gather public facts.** Company website, a recent news story, the annual report or investor page, the buyer's LinkedIn profile. For consumer brands, add customer reviews.
2. **Ask for a one-page brief.** "Using only this material…": what they do, top three priorities, recent changes, key people, open questions. Ask it to mark anything uncertain with (?).
3. **Turn it into questions.** "Give me three opening questions that show I understand their situation."
4. **Verify, then call.** Check every fact you plan to say out loud. One wrong fact undoes all the preparation.

**Tip:** many assistants can now search the web for you. That's useful, but ask for the source of every claim and click through to check it.

## Real-world example

**Lumen Technologies**, a US communications company, rolled out Microsoft's Copilot to its sellers. In Microsoft's customer story, Lumen's Chief Revenue Officer says research for customer outreach typically took a seller **four hours**, and with generative AI it now takes about **15 minutes**. She values getting four hours back each week at **$50 million in revenue** over a year.

These are company-reported figures in a vendor's case study, not an independent audit. But the direction is clear, and you can test it on your own accounts this week.

**The lesson for leaders:** make AI-assisted call prep a team standard, with one rule that never bends: verify before you say it.
