## The idea

AI can draft a blog post in seconds and turn it into ten social posts in seconds more. That's a superpower and a risk. Without direction, you get **more content that sounds like everyone else's**.

The fix is simple: humans do the **thinking** (the brief, the brand rules, the proof) and the **final check**. AI does the drafting and the repurposing.

## Why it matters

- **Speed without sameness.** A clear brief and brand rules keep AI output distinctive.
- **One idea, many channels.** Repurposing gets more value from every strong piece of research or story.
- **Claims carry risk.** A made-up statistic or overstated promise in marketing copy is still your company's responsibility.

## How it works

1. **Write a 5-line brief.** Audience, goal, key message, one proof point, call to action. This is where the strategy lives.
2. **Add your brand rules.** Paste your voice card (Day 7) and a short list of words to avoid and claims you can't make. Style guides like Mailchimp's show what good rules look like.
3. **Draft, then repurpose.** Ask for the main piece first. Then: "Turn this into a LinkedIn post, a 3-line email teaser and a 30-second video script." Each version should fit its channel.
4. **Edit and check claims.** Read everything. Check every fact, number and promise against a source before it's published.

**Tip:** save your brief template and brand rules as a project, custom GPT or Gem (Day 10) so every draft starts on-brand.

## Real-world example

*An illustrative scenario.* A B2B services firm's two-person marketing team used to publish one article a month. They now write one brief per week, let AI draft the article in their voice card, and repurpose each into a newsletter section, three LinkedIn posts and talking points for sales.

Output quadruples, but the bigger change is consistency: sales and marketing use the same message every week. One rule never changes: a named person checks every claim before anything goes live. A consumer food brand runs the same playbook for recipes, adapting each one for Instagram, email and in-store cards.

**The lesson for leaders:** invest in briefs and brand rules, not just tools. That's what turns AI speed into brand strength.
