## The idea

Most leaders spend more time formatting slides than sharpening the message. AI can flip that. It can help you build a **storyline**, turn notes or documents into a **slide-by-slide outline**, and produce a **first draft deck** in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

Your time then goes into what matters: the message, the evidence and the ask.

## Why it matters

- **Hours saved.** A first draft from a document or outline takes minutes, not an afternoon.
- **Clearer decks.** Starting from a storyline forces one message per slide.
- **More rehearsal time.** Time saved on building can go into practising the delivery.

## How it works

1. **Build the storyline.** Who's the audience? What do you want them to do? Ask AI to help you find **one main message** and **three supporting points**.
2. **Outline with headlines.** Ask for a slide-by-slide outline where each title states the point ("Costs fell faster than sales"), not the topic ("Costs").
3. **Draft the slides.** Microsoft 365: Copilot in PowerPoint can create a presentation from a prompt or a Word document. Google Workspace: Gemini in Google Slides can help create slides and images. Neither? Paste the outline into your template.
4. **Edit for clarity and accuracy.** Cut anything that doesn't support the message. Check every number and claim against the source.

**Tip:** ask AI to play your audience (Day 9): "You're our CFO. What three questions will you ask after this deck?"

## Real-world example

*An illustrative scenario.* A sales director needs a quarterly business review deck for a major customer by tomorrow. She pastes her account notes and results into an approved assistant and asks for a storyline: "We delivered savings; here's how; here's what we'll do next."

AI proposes an 8-slide outline with headline titles. She drafts it with Copilot in PowerPoint, replaces two generic slides with real customer data, and fixes one percentage that was misread from the notes. The deck takes 90 minutes instead of a day, and she spends the extra time rehearsing the pricing conversation.

**The lesson for leaders:** ask your team for the storyline before the slides. AI builds the deck; people own the message and the numbers.
