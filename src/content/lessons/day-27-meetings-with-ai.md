## The idea

Meetings take up a growing share of the working week, and many end without clear decisions. AI can help at all three stages: a **sharper agenda before**, **automatic notes during**, and a **clear recap after**.

Copilot in Microsoft Teams and Gemini in Google Meet can transcribe and summarise meetings. Any assistant can help write the agenda and recap from your notes.

## Why it matters

- **Meetings are the top complaint.** In Microsoft's 2023 Work Trend Index, people rated inefficient meetings as their number one productivity disruptor.
- **Decisions get lost.** Without a written recap, people leave with different memories of what was agreed.
- **Missed meetings are costly.** AI catch-up summaries help you skip a meeting and still know what matters.

## How it works

1. **Sharper agenda.** Ask AI to turn your notes into an agenda with a purpose for each item (decide, discuss or inform), time limits and owners. Put decisions first.
2. **Consent, then notes.** Tell everyone if AI will transcribe or summarise. Follow your company's policy and local law (Day 14).
3. **Decisions and actions.** After the meeting, ask for: decisions made, actions with one owner and a due date, and open questions.
4. **Same-day recap, checked.** Read it, fix anything wrong (AI can mishear names and numbers), then send within the hour.

**Which tool?** Microsoft 365: Copilot in Teams can recap meetings that have a transcript. Google Workspace: Gemini in Meet can take notes for eligible plans. Neither? Use your own notes plus any assistant.

## Real-world example

Microsoft's **2023 Work Trend Index** combined a survey of 31,000 people with Microsoft 365 usage data. Inefficient meetings came out as the top productivity disruptor, and 68% of people said they lack enough uninterrupted focus time. More meetings means less time for deep work.

*An illustrative scenario:* a leadership team cuts its weekly meeting from 90 to 45 minutes. Agendas are drafted with AI and label every item decide, discuss or inform. Recaps go out within the hour, listing owners and dates, and are checked by the chief of staff. Within a month, the same actions stop reappearing week after week.

**The lesson for leaders:** no agenda, no meeting; no recap, no decision. Let AI do the writing; people own the decisions.
